Slothbloom Reader Account And Data Deletion

Slothbloom Reader primarily stores your reading data locally on your device. This page explains how to remove local app data and how to request deletion of any account-linked or cloud-linked data if that applies to your use of the app.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Delete Local App Data On Android

If you only used Slothbloom Reader locally, you can remove your reading data directly from the device:

  1. Open Slothbloom Reader.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Use the local reset or data-clearing controls if available.
  4. You can also remove the app and clear its storage from Android system app settings.

This will remove locally stored readings, saved study content, preferences, and similar device-only app data.

Delete Local App Data On iPhone Or iPad

Export anything you want to keep before deleting local data. To remove Reader and its on-device app data, open the device Settings, choose General, then iPhone Storage or iPad Storage. Select Reader and choose Delete App.

Offload App keeps documents and data. Removing the icon from the Home Screen also keeps the app. Exported files, shared copies and device backups are separate and may need to be removed from their own locations.

Removing the app does not delete your Reader sign-in account, cloud AI results or subscriptions. See Apple’s storage guide for the difference between deleting and offloading an app.

Delete Your Reader Account On iPhone Or iPad

If your installed version offers account deletion, open Reader Settings, open your account, then Account and cloud data and Delete account. Read the details and complete the fresh Apple or Google sign-in requested to confirm your identity.

Successful account deletion removes your Reader sign-in account, cloud library and saved cloud AI results, and removes access to account credits. Local readings remain on the device. Purchase, credit and security records are retained to prevent fraud and duplicate claims. This action cannot be undone.

If deletion is unavailable, fails, or you cannot sign in, contact support@slothbloom.com for help. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or copies of your books and recordings.

Request Deletion Of Account-Linked Or Cloud-Linked Data

If you used a cloud-linked, account-linked, support-linked, or purchase-support workflow and want any associated data reviewed for deletion, send a request to support@slothbloom.com.

Please include:

  • Your email address used for the request or related support contact
  • The app name: Slothbloom Reader
  • Your request type: local data question, account deletion, or cloud-data deletion
  • Any helpful details that identify the data to remove

What Happens After A Deletion Request

We will review the request and remove account-linked or cloud-linked data that is reasonably tied to your request and not required to be retained for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, security, or operational reasons.

Some records, such as transaction, tax, chargeback, fraud-prevention, or basic support logs, may need to be retained for a limited period where required.

Subscription And Purchase Notes

Deleting the app or your Reader account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel through the store that bills you:

  • Apple: Open your device Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select Reader Plus and cancel if you no longer want it to renew. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.
  • Google Play: Manage Reader Plus through your Google Play subscriptions page.

Account deletion does not erase Apple or Google purchase records or automatically refund purchases. Contact support if you need help identifying which store billed you.

Questions

If you are not sure whether your data is only local or includes account-linked support or purchase information, contact support@slothbloom.com and we can help you identify the right deletion path.
