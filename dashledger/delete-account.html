Account Deletion

Updated September 11, 2026

Privacy PolicyAccount Deletion

You can request deletion of your DashLedger account and associated data without installing or signing in to the app.

Request by email

Email support@slothbloom.com with the subject DashLedger account deletion. Include the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password, payment-card details or identity documents. We may contact you to verify account ownership and confirm the request.

Request in the app

If your installed version supports it, open Account → Sign-in and security → Delete account and data. Finish or cancel active trips and end any open shift. Review the warning and confirm. A recent sign-in may be required. If the server is unavailable or the feature is not enabled, use the email route above.

Scope and exceptions

Account deletion removes the DashLedger account and associated profile, work and financial records, cloud snapshots and receipt files held by DashLedger, subject to any required legal or security retention described in the privacy policy. In-app cleanup also removes that account's records from the device where it completes. Externally saved exports and copies on other devices must be removed separately. Payment providers manage their own purchase records.

Before deleting

Export records you need to keep. Deletion cannot be undone. Account deletion does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play if you do not want it to renew. If an in-app deletion is interrupted, keep the app installed and use its retry option or contact support.
